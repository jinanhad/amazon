# KDP listing plan

Everything here is a draft to be finalised once the manuscript exists. Items marked **verify** depend on Amazon rules that change; check them in your KDP account before publishing, not from this file.

## Title options

Amazon search weighs the title and subtitle heavily, so both should carry the words a buyer types.

1. **Bookkeeping and VAT for Small Business Owners**
   *A Plain-Language Guide to Keeping Your Books, Filing VAT, and Working in Two Currencies*
2. **Small Business Bookkeeping Made Simple**
   *Double-Entry, VAT, and Multi-Currency Accounting Explained for Non-Accountants*
3. **Your Books, Your Business**
   *Bookkeeping and VAT for Owners Who Would Rather Be Doing Anything Else*

Recommendation: option 1. It is the most searchable. Option 3 is the most memorable but loses search traffic.

## Author name

Your real name, or a consistent pen name. Decide before publishing; changing it later loses reviews. If you plan to link the book to your software, use the name you trade under.

## Seven keywords (draft)

KDP allows seven keyword phrases. Do not repeat words already in the title. Draft:

1. small business accounting for beginners
2. VAT accounting guide
3. double entry bookkeeping explained
4. multi currency accounting
5. bookkeeping for freelancers and self employed
6. how to read a balance sheet and profit and loss
7. month end close checklist

Refine these by typing each into the Amazon search bar and watching the autocomplete suggestions. Real buyer phrasing beats guesses.

## Categories

KDP lets you pick up to three categories. **Verify** the exact category tree at publish time. Target the narrowest categories where the book can realistically rank, not the broadest:

- Business & Money > Accounting > Bookkeeping
- Business & Money > Taxation > Small Business
- Business & Money > Small Business & Entrepreneurship > Home-Based

## Pricing

- Launch at **4.99 USD** for the first weeks to gather reviews, then move to **6.99 or 7.99 USD**.
- 70 percent royalty band. Several sources reported in 2026 that Amazon raised the ceiling for the 70 percent band to 12.99 USD and that this is opt-in per title. **Verify** on the KDP eBook Royalties help page; I could not reach it from this environment.
- Delivery fee is deducted per sale based on file size. Keep images small.

## KDP Select

Recommendation: **enrol for the first 90 days.** Kindle Unlimited page reads are a real income source for how-to nonfiction, and the free promotion days help gather early reviews. You can leave after 90 days if you want to sell the ebook elsewhere.

## Cover brief

- Ebook cover, JPEG, portrait, roughly 1600 by 2560 pixels. **Verify** current KDP cover specs.
- Must read at thumbnail size: a big title, high contrast, no fine detail.
- Look at the top 20 books in the Bookkeeping category and match their visual language enough that yours looks like it belongs, then differ in colour.
- Do it in Canva yourself, or brief a designer on Fiverr. A reasonable brief is this file plus three covers you like.

## Formatting and upload

- Write in Markdown in this folder, one file per chapter.
- Convert to EPUB with Pandoc, or paste into Kindle Create for the KPF format.
- Check in Kindle Previewer on phone, tablet and e-reader layouts before publishing.
- Tables render badly on e-readers. Keep them short or turn them into lists.

## AI disclosure answer

When KDP asks, the honest answer depends on how we work. If you supply the substance and Claude edits, the answer is "no AI-generated content" for text. If a chapter ends up substantially drafted by Claude, declare it. Cover images made with an AI image tool must be declared as AI-generated images. **Verify** the current wording of the KDP question at publish time.

## Legal and account setup

- Tax interview during account setup. Payments and withholding depend on your country and its tax treaty with the US. **Verify** for your country.
- Kindle store availability in Lebanon is limited, so expect most sales from the US, UK, Gulf and European stores. Price accordingly in each marketplace.
- Add a short disclaimer: the book is general education, not tax advice, and local rules change.

## Launch checklist

1. Manuscript final, proofread once by a human.
2. Cover final and checked at thumbnail size.
3. KDP account, tax interview and bank details complete.
4. Listing text, keywords and categories entered.
5. Preview checked on three device layouts.
6. Enrol in KDP Select, set launch price.
7. Line up five to ten early readers to review in the first two weeks.
8. Add a page at the back of the book pointing to the next title and to your software.
