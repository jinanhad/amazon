## Description (draft)

> When a feeling is too big to hold, a small golden light appears.
>
> Noor is eight. Some nights the thunder is too loud, or the chair by the window is too empty, or the worry cloud will not leave. That is when Fanoos the firefly comes, and together they visit the Lantern Garden, where every creature carries a feeling of its own.
>
> In eight gentle bedtime stories, Noor helps a frightened rabbit, a grieving goose, a rumbling mountain and more, and in helping them she remembers what her grandmother taught her: one of Allah's beautiful names, one example from the Prophet and his family, peace be upon them, and one small thing she can do herself.
>
> Each story ends with questions to talk about together, something to try tonight, a short dua with meaning, and a note for grown-ups.
>
> Written for Shia Muslim families, with stories of Imam Ali, Sayyida Fatima, Sayyida Zaynab and the tasbih of Fatima woven through. For ages 6 to 10, to read together.
>
> Feelings covered: fear of loud noises, grief, anger, worry, helplessness, moving home, nightmares, and jealousy.

## Illustrations, the main cost

- Children's books do not sell without pictures. Plan one full-page illustration per story plus a cover, minimum nine; sixteen is better.
- Style: warm, lantern-lit, soft. Noor in modest dress with hijab optional at her age, decide with your family's norms. The Prophet and the Imams are never depicted; the stories are written so no scene requires it.
- Options: a freelance illustrator on Fiverr or Upwork, typically 30 to 150 US dollars per illustration for this style, so budget 400 to 1,500 US dollars. I am not certain of current rates; get three quotes. AI-generated illustrations are cheaper but must be declared on KDP and many parents in this market dislike them.
- Ebook: use Kindle Create or the Kindle Kids' Book Creator for fixed-layout picture books. **Verify** which tool KDP currently recommends.

## Formats and pricing

- **Paperback first.** Parents buy children's books to hold. Print on demand through KDP, no upfront cost. Price around 9.99 to 12.99 US dollars for a 60 to 80 page colour paperback; check KDP's printing cost calculator, colour printing is expensive and the royalty may be thin. **Verify.**
- **Ebook** at 3.99 to 4.99 US dollars. Picture-book ebooks sell less than the print edition but are cheap to add.
- **Hardcover** later if the paperback sells.

## KDP Select

Enrol the ebook for the first 90 days. Kindle Unlimited reads are meaningful for children's books because parents read many.

## Before publishing

1. Religious review by a qualified Shia scholar, every dua and hadith checked, reviewer named in the parents' note.
2. Child psychology review of the grown-up notes.
3. Read aloud to at least three children in the age range, and watch where they lose interest. Cut those lines.
4. Illustrations final, cover checked at thumbnail size.
5. KDP account, tax interview and bank details complete.
6. Proof copy of the paperback ordered and checked in hand before going live.

## Launch

- Islamic schools, husseiniyas, and mosque children's programmes are the natural first readers. Offer a free copy to a few for review.
- Shia parenting groups on Facebook, WhatsApp and Instagram in the UK, US, Canada and Australia.
- Time the launch away from Muharram, when families are busy, or lean into it with story 2 if you prefer.
- Arabic edition as book two of the same title, then the next set of feelings.
